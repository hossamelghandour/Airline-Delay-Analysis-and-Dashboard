# ✈️ Airline Delays Dashboard

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of airline delays across various carriers and airports. It helps users understand the frequency, causes, and patterns of flight delays and cancellations in a clear and visual manner.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard consists of **three main pages**:

---

### 1. **Flight Overview**

This section provides a high-level summary of flight operations:

- **Total Flights:** 2 million
- **Total Carriers:** 20
- **Total Airports:** 306
- **Cancelled Flights:** 635
- **Diverted Flights:** 7,790

#### Visual Insights:

- **Top 10 Airlines by Number of Flights**: Southwest Airlines leads with the most flights.
- **Monthly Flight Trends**: Peak months are **March, June, and December**, while **September and October** show the least traffic.
- **Flights by Airport Map**: Geographic distribution of flights across the world.

---

### 2. **Delay Analysis**

This page analyzes the types and frequencies of flight delays.

#### Delay Types:

- **Weather Delay:** 598
- **Security Delay:** 155
- **NAS Delay (National Airspace System):** 573
- **Late Aircraft Delay:** 563
- **Carrier Delay:** 982

#### Key Visuals:

- **Pie Chart:** Breakdown of delay types by percentage.
- **Bar Chart by Carrier:** Compares Weather, Security, and NAS delays across airlines.
- **Top 10 Delayed Airports:** O’Hare, Atlanta, and Dallas-Fort Worth top the list.
- **Monthly Delay Trends:** December shows the highest delay numbers.

---

### 3. **Performance Metrics**

This section focuses on the performance of airlines based on delay durations.

#### KPIs:

- **Average Arrival Delay:** 42.23 minutes
- **Average Departure Delay:** 43.22 minutes
- **Max Arrival Delay:** 2,461 minutes
- **Max Departure Delay:** 2,467 minutes

#### Visual Insights:

- **Top 5 Carriers by Avg Arrival Delay:** Mesa Airlines, Comair, and JetBlue show the highest average delays.
- **Delay Comparison by Carrier:** Visual comparison of arrival and departure delays.
- **Monthly Delay Trend:** Delay durations peak in **June** and **December**.

---

## 🔍 Filters

- **Airport Selector:** Analyze data for a specific airport.
- **Month Selector:** Drill down to a specific month for trend analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Power BI** for interactive data visualization
- **DAX & Power Query** for data modeling and transformation
- **Microsoft Bing Maps** integration for geographical visuals

---



## 📌 Key Insights

- December is the busiest month with the highest number of flights and delays.
- Southwest Airlines operates the most flights.
- O’Hare International Airport experiences the most delays.
- Carrier delays are the most common delay type.
